The regional map retains the frozen R boundary and Natural Earth geography. The proposal map illustrates the original research scope. Sea motion, props and applications are visual illustrations, not observed events, physical drift simulations or model forecasts. Current validation concerns drifters.

Core references

  • Brolly (2023), Inferring Ocean Transport Statistics with Probabilistic Neural Networks. Continuous location-conditioned transition densities; Gaussian mixtures and MDNs. Its global four-day results are not directly comparable with our regional seven-day scores.
  • Miron et al. (2017), Lagrangian Dynamical Geography of the Gulf of Mexico. Markov-chain representation, empirical transitions and regional connectivity. We borrow the transport representation; we do not claim to reproduce its dynamical provinces.

Technical design references

  • Chamberlain et al. (2023): grid and time-step tradeoffs, sampling uncertainty and repeated composition.
  • O’Malley et al. (2021): grid arrangement and coordinate rotation sensitivity. The paper's rotation experiments are not presented as completed project experiments.
  • Pope, Stephenson & Jackson (2020): temporal forgetting of older transitions. Design reference, not an implemented model.
