Preprocessing distinctions

  • Drogue: cleaned status is 1=attached, 0=lost, −1=unknown. Source loss-date zero means unknown, not cleaned status zero.
  • SST: mostly same-date NOAA OISST background. Four valid nodes support bilinear reconstruction; sparse-node cases use the approved alternatives. These are background estimates, not recovered hourly measurements. The SST product grid is separate from the modelling grid.
  • Motion: retain native ve/vn and derive interval motion from actual timestamps; no invented missing positions.
  • Recovery: remove rows strictly after valid official last-good cutoffs. Eventual grounding: set later SST to NA while preserving position/time records for audit. Uncertain events remain uncertain.
  • Hardware illustration is not to scale and does not imply all observations share a fixed drogue depth. Final MDN input is longitude and latitude only.

R, D and the endpoint

R is the frozen Southern Corridor departure polygon. D is the early grid-model domain: 81°W–8°W, 20°N–55°N. In the stopped grid task, observed first exit is an E outcome, not simply deleted data. Continuous endpoint probability is not clipped to D.

Annual support and final roles

Bars reproduce the sealed historical development-label audit dated 2 November: 1° stopped labels, reason_code=0, seven-day horizon, grouped by origin year before final chronological and whole-ID exclusions.
