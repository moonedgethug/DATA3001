# What ocean transport studies can teach our endpoint forecasts

5 November 2026 · DATA3001 research discussion · Targeted primary-source review and retrospective analysis of saved development predictions.

## The decision

A release occurs at a known origin. We need probabilities for its stopped destination at day 7 or day 30, including first exit from the study domain. A useful change should reduce paired Brier loss while preserving actual 90% region hits and geographic region size. A study of transport routes, a density forecast and a retrospective reconstruction can each inform this question, but their published accuracy measures answer different questions.

### The mathematical distinction

Our direct empirical estimate uses a separate matrix at each requested horizon. With training count $N_{ij}(h)$ from origin $i$ to outcome $j$, its probability is $N_{ij}(h)/N_i(h)$. Reweighting these records by their heading or speed can redistribute mass among recorded destinations. A destination with no contributing record still receives zero.

Miron's propagation starts with an estimated short-lag matrix $P_\tau$. Two steps give $(P_\tau^2)_{ij}=\sum_k P_{ik}P_{kj}$. Observations connecting $i$ to $k$ and $k$ to $j$ can support $i$ to $j$. This requires the distribution of the next step at $k$ to be adequately represented by that row, regardless of the route to $k$. Repeated steps can accumulate bias if entering the same box from different currents implies different onward motion. The project already has lag, exact-position and schedule comparisons. The historical 30-day position-versus-coarse paired interval included zero. [Miron et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1703.10684) · [Project iteration report](../results/runs/markov_iteration_cv_20261027T120356_034230Z/REPORT.md)

Brolly models $p_\tau(\delta\mid x)=\sum_{k=1}^K\pi_k(x)\,\mathcal N(\delta;\mu_k(x),\Sigma_k(x))$. Here $x$ is the starting position, $\delta$ is displacement, and the component means, spreads and weights describe possible directions and distances. Training maximises the log density of observed displacements. Brolly motivates the four-day choice with prior estimates of surface-velocity decorrelation around two to three days. In simulation each draw starts the next step at its newly reached position. The global validation log scores in Table 1 are −1.74 (matrix), −1.30 (gridded Gaussian), −1.14 (one-component network) and −1.11 (32 components). Higher is better. The matrix score omits about 7% of global validation displacements that it assigned zero probability. The split held out whole drifters at random, and simulation redrew land endpoints. These distinctions prevent a numerical performance comparison with our chronological all-case Brier and first-exit definition. [Paper, §§4.1–4.4.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2303.11480) · [Implementation](https://github.com/mtbrolly/transition_density)

For a different way of adding information, Liu et al. advected particles with altimetry-derived currents in the eastern Gulf of Mexico. Adding a wind-driven Ekman component helped in their hindcast, particularly over the shelf. Their outcome was trajectory separation and skill. For a prospective release forecast we would need a current observation available at release, or a forecast issued by then. Later analysed currents would answer a retrospective question. [Liu et al. (2014)](https://doi.org/10.1002/2013JC009710)

### Where are the missed destinations?

I reconciled the saved annual probability rows and case scores for all 33,971 seven-day and 30,128 thirty-day departures. For each observed destination I measured its minimum grid distance to any destination with positive annual probability for the same origin and horizon. Distance one means sharing an edge or corner. This is geographic proximity on the 1° grid. It does not establish a navigable connection, and cells are not equal-sized in kilometres. Exit was kept distinct and no observed case in this block was an exit.

As a separate check, I linked the 30-day E2 forward-policy LocalEndpointAdaptive scores to the same observations by ID, horizon, origin and destination and reconciled their multiplicities. ## Recommended decision

1. **Retain the seven-day heading result.** E5's proposed scalar-speed contrast still answers a worthwhile, narrow question. Its positive donor votes preserve annual support. A release described only by its position may have no observed past-hour velocity. [Speed design](departure_speed_s4_e5_plan.md)
2. **For 30 days, prioritise one geographically constrained change on the destination side.** Starting with the annual B2 row, leave most probability on recorded destinations and allow a training-selected fraction to move to immediately adjoining water cells. Preserve exit mass and stop probability from crossing a land barrier. One possible form is $p_\eta=(1-\eta)p_0+\eta p_0 K$, where the row-stochastic $K$ acts on destinations and fixes exit. With no eligible neighbour, the row stays at its own destination. This is a proposed spatial estimator, not a physical trajectory simulator. The fraction of near misses above is an upper bound on geographic opportunity before water-connectivity and mass constraints. It gives no lower bound on future hits. Discrete-kernel smoothing with cross-validated parameters has precedent in sparse transition estimation. The marine kernel and the decision criterion here would be ours. [Kang & Rey](https://doi.org/10.1111/gean.12222)
3. **Judge the extra assumption on its own.** Use identical releases, outcomes, equal-ID/equal-origin scores and fixed 90% mass. Include zero smoothing, tune only inside forward training windows, keep annual B2 as the matched parent and LocalEndpointAdaptive as a strong reference. Report paired Brier differences, H90, K90, zero-truth mass, uncertainty and the frequency of inaccessible neighbour candidates. Claim a quality-preserving gain against a named reference only if Brier improves, H90 is preserved and K90 does not grow under predeclared margins. If improving on B2 requires a much larger region, report the trade-off. Repeated inspection of 2020–2022 makes it development evidence. [Existing evaluation contract](departure_direction_s4_e4_plan.md)
4. **If the local allowance mostly spreads the forecast, reorient toward information that can pick the active flow.** A dated surface-current vector or a jet-location indicator available at release would give two otherwise identical starts different predictions. Audit coverage and time of availability first. A short-lag propagation study would also need a direct-versus-composed comparison on identical stopped targets before adopting a step size. Neither alternative requires assuming an advantage in advance. [Physical hindcast example](https://doi.org/10.1002/2013JC009710) · [Historical continuous-model diagnostics](../results/deliverables/continuous_diagnostics/REPORT.md)

The diagnostic uses existing development forecasts. This review adds no fitted estimator, changes no experiment contract and makes no claim about the locked final test.
