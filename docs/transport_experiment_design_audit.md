# What experiments did Brolly and Miron actually design?

5 November 2026 · Research design review · Source reading only

**Decision.** Keep direct B2 and the five-stage framework. Defer the E6 wind acquisition and fitting proposal. The draft can test whether recent wind sorts an origin cell's *observed* destinations into more predictive groups. It cannot test the broader claim that evolving wind changes ocean transport. The distinction matters most at 30 days.

## Brolly: compare representations of one transition law

The 2023 paper estimates the distribution of four-day displacement conditional on departure coordinates. The author motivates the horizon using prior estimates of velocity decorrelation. The submission repository records a request for drogued GDP observations. Its preprocessing removes entire IDs with gaps in the six-hour series, drops temperature and velocity columns and splits whole IDs approximately in half. Its pair builder supports overlapping windows by default. The paper reports 23,893 trajectories and 11,946 assigned to model building. That experiment does not use our pooled population or chronological prediction setting. [Paper, §§4.1–4.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2303.11480) · [Submission release](https://github.com/mtbrolly/transition_density/releases/tag/v1.0.0) · [Data selection, ID split and pairing](https://github.com/mtbrolly/transition_density/blob/544fa61ad4ca1c69f7383fdfbf3539eaa379d23b/drifter_data.py#L52-L140) · [Original drogue request](https://github.com/mtbrolly/transition_density/blob/544fa61ad4ca1c69f7383fdfbf3539eaa379d23b/drifter_data.py#L24-L35)

The comparisons progressively change the form of the conditional distribution: cell counts, one Gaussian per origin cell, and one or 32 Gaussian components varying with exact location. Figure 8 examines two specific releases, one in the Gulf Stream core and one outside. The former has elongated, non-Gaussian contours; the latter is closer to Gaussian. The figure illustrates the model's learned shape. Table 1 separately assesses held-out log density. Globally the single- and 32-component networks score −1.14 and −1.11. These results motivate examining *where* a richer distribution makes a difference. They do not establish the need for 32 components locally. [Paper, Table 1 and Figure 8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2303.11480#page=14) · [Density training and objective](https://github.com/mtbrolly/transition_density/blob/544fa61ad4ca1c69f7383fdfbf3539eaa379d23b/modelling.py#L58-L117)

Two details affect transfer. The regional comparison script filters both starting and ending positions to the selected region; the simulation redraws proposals ending on land. Our stopped process must preserve first exit even if the terminal position would return inside. Also, the paper reports excluding zero-density cases from the matrix log score (about 7% globally). The submission code's optional masking line is commented out, and the comparison script ships with a particular region and resolution sweep. Running those defaults does not reproduce every published table entry. We inspected tagged source without running it. [Regional comparison](https://github.com/mtbrolly/transition_density/blob/544fa61ad4ca1c69f7383fdfbf3539eaa379d23b/model_comparison.py#L15-L76) · [Matrix log score](https://github.com/mtbrolly/transition_density/blob/544fa61ad4ca1c69f7383fdfbf3539eaa379d23b/grids.py#L206-L236) · [Simulation and land rule](https://github.com/mtbrolly/transition_density/blob/544fa61ad4ca1c69f7383fdfbf3539eaa379d23b/simulate_drifters.py#L55-L82)

## Miron: compare predicted transport with an observable physical pattern

**Gulf of Mexico, 2017.** The team constructed two-day transition rows from 3,207 drifters on roughly 50 km cells and identified weakly exchanging regions from the matrix. The most relevant check for DATA3001 is the pair of figures 7 and 8. They chose release sites on opposite sides of inferred transport boundaries, propagated probability from those sites, then directly counted where drifters passing through the same sites appeared a week and a month later. These maps check whether the inferred separation is visible in the trajectories. The direct counts are independent of matrix iteration as a calculation; the paper does not identify them as a disjoint future test set. It also reports sensitivity to lag/data removal and looks for corroboration in other observations. [Methods and interpretation](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1703.10684) · [Modelled releases, Figure 7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1703.10684#page=15) · [Observed releases, Figure 8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1703.10684#page=16)

**Indian Ocean/MH370, 2019.** The authors used undrogued segments for the floating debris target. Before adopting a five-day operator, they compared eigenvalues from directly estimated longer lags with powers of eigenvalues from a one-day matrix. Agreement through ten daily steps supported their scale choice. This spectral comparison alone does not certify individual rows. They compared an annual operator with seasonally ordered operators built around the Indian monsoon. A particle still at sea can encounter a new matrix when the calendar changes. Observed debris landfalls and times subsequently inform a Bayesian *inverse* estimate of source. The endpoint question and the available evidence differ from ours. [Paper, §§III–V](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1903.06165)

**What the toolbox implements.** The author's pygtm README lists the Gulf of Mexico, MH370 and later debris papers. The 2021 debris study explicitly cites the code. In release v0.1 the segment builder pairs positions at a specified lag, the matrix builder counts destinations by origin, and the push-forward repeatedly multiplies a row distribution by the matrix. Its North Atlantic tutorial uses drogued trajectories; the MH370 paper selected undrogued trajectories. Treat this as a shared toolbox with application-specific choices. The December 2021 release is not a frozen copy of the 2017 execution. The 2021 garbage-patch paper also incorporates loss and reinjection, appropriate to a continuing pollution source. [Toolbox and paper list](https://github.com/philippemiron/pygtm#used-in-the-following-publications) · [Segments](https://github.com/philippemiron/pygtm/blob/fa9803816c90efaf66a871e218e7b96c9967dd6b/pygtm/dataset.py#L83-L111) · [Matrix counts](https://github.com/philippemiron/pygtm/blob/fa9803816c90efaf66a871e218e7b96c9967dd6b/pygtm/matrix.py#L30-L90) · [Push-forward](https://github.com/philippemiron/pygtm/blob/fa9803816c90efaf66a871e218e7b96c9967dd6b/pygtm/matrix.py#L208-L223) · [Tutorial](https://github.com/philippemiron/pygtm/blob/6ed86e722daca3cb10da6b9df72b9c4eb4a3bbd5/tutorials/05-push_forward.ipynb) · [2021 paper](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0030535)

## Where Miron actually used wind

Two different experiments help define a question about wind. In a 2020 North Atlantic field study, four deployments included replicates of differently shaped objects in similar initial conditions. The authors compared trajectory clustering by design and evaluated an inertial drift model using observed paths and separation distances. Performance varied by design and deployment. This provides a reason to specify which object is being forecast. It does not validate one wind coefficient for pooled GDP drifters. [Miron et al. 2020, methods and Figure 4](https://doi.org/10.1029/2020GL089874)

In the separate Caribbean litter repository, the runner can turn wind on or off while retaining the ocean-current field. With wind enabled it adds scaled JRA55 winds to HYCOM currents. Particle integration samples velocities at changing locations and times. The example config sets the multiplier to 0.01. The README identifies this as a Caribbean adaptation of a different marine-litter project. It is an implementation example, not an MH370 routine, a fitted coefficient for our drifters, or a published Brier improvement. Archived future environmental fields describe a hindcast unless an operational forecast issued by release time is used. [Project provenance](https://github.com/philippemiron/marine-litter-caribbean/blob/cbf19ab4c5988b0d2f725e68ee4dbfcc86fb58f6/README.md#L1-L39) · [Wind on/off](https://github.com/philippemiron/marine-litter-caribbean/blob/cbf19ab4c5988b0d2f725e68ee4dbfcc86fb58f6/caribbean_model.py#L38-L51) · [Scaling](https://github.com/philippemiron/marine-litter-caribbean/blob/cbf19ab4c5988b0d2f725e68ee4dbfcc86fb58f6/fields.py#L125-L150) · [Particle integration](https://github.com/philippemiron/marine-litter-caribbean/blob/cbf19ab4c5988b0d2f725e68ee4dbfcc86fb58f6/kernels.py#L16-L37)

| Can wind repair missing 30-day destinations? | For annual row A, every wind-group row R uses a subset of A's records. Under the E6 blend, A[j] = 0 implies R[j] = 0 and the forecast is also zero. On the latest development block the observed destination has zero annual probability for 26.86% of evaluation weight. | The E6 comparison can detect a better ranking of existing destinations. It is structurally unable to assign mass to any of the missing ones. Brier can still change on those cases through the concentration of the other probabilities. Training populations and evaluation policy differ from Step 4. The old result motivates a matched comparison, not a numerical ranking against the later chronological scores. Matched draws retained some true-season cases, and only 38.2% of target weight met the informative-subset definition. | A worse grouped model cannot establish absence of a physical effect. **Recommended order, subject to discussion.** First use the saved forecasts to make the equivalent of Miron's paired maps: choose origins using geography and training support, show the empirical held-out destinations beside each model on identical cases and scales, and report how many distinct drifters contribute. Diagnose missing support, displacement of the main route and excessive spread separately. Label maps of a small validation cohort accordingly, use all origins for primary scores, and keep observed first exit explicit. This is a proposed review of existing evidence. No such new analysis was run for this memo.

If the main failure is missing nearby water cells, a constrained change to the destination distribution has a testable purpose. If the main route itself moves, increasing footprint can disguise the error. Inspect whether qualifying short-lag movements from the same cell differ consistently across independent drifters, times and relevant object statuses. Only then specify whether a wind study asks about information at departure, a time-changing operator, or physically forced trajectories. For predicting a future release, any forcing later on the path must come from a forecast available at departure. These choices preserve the five-stage framework and avoid selecting an estimator using the same development-period scores presented as confirmation.
