# PT1 visual sources

Sky environment: Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy (Pure Sky), original Greg Zaal, sky edits Jarod Guest; Poly Haven CC0. https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky ; https://polyhaven.com/license . 2K HDR copied unchanged. Used as artistic daylight environment, not the observed weather of a research case.

HDRLoader: Three.js 0.181.1, MIT, https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/three@0.181.1/examples/jsm/loaders/HDRLoader.js . Existing THREE-LICENSE.txt applies.

Sea waves, buoy, bottle, paper, book, beach, raft, family silhouettes, containers and surface slick are authored visual illustrations. They do not reconstruct a recorded event or a model forecast.

Regional geographic context and original frozen R: existing assets/geography.json and release_R.geojson; see assets/SOURCES.md. Proposal map is a scope schematic, not a predicted trajectory.

Sand PBR material: Coast Sand 01 by Rob Tuytel, Poly Haven, CC0. https://polyhaven.com/a/coast_sand_01 . Original color, normal, roughness, AO and displacement maps; download hashes and URLs are in sand/provenance.json.

Core paper titles and roles were rechecked against https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.11480 and https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.10684 on 2026-11-16. Project method wording is grounded in the existing 20261114 narrative audit.
