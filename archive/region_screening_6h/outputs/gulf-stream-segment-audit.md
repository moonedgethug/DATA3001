# Gulf Stream Candidate-Window Audit

## Purpose

This audit tests whether a bounded part of the broad Gulf Stream Corridor is a more defensible first research target than the full corridor or a data-weaker source region.

The candidate geometries are analytical screening windows. They are non-overlapping, but they do not claim to partition the full physical current or define official current boundaries.

## Candidate windows

- **Southern Coastal Segment:** the existing corridor intersected with 30.0–35.5°N and 82–73°W. This covers the Florida-to-Carolinas approach and is the most directly connected to the Florida-Bahamas transition and US East Coast applications.
- **Cape Hatteras Separation:** the existing corridor intersected with 35.5–38.5°N and 78–66°W. This covers the separation/turning region and is a useful dynamic stress test.
- **Northern Offshore Extension:** the existing corridor intersected with 38.5–42.5°N and 74–60°W. This is strongly supported but farther offshore and less directly aligned with the initial coastal application framing.

## Interpretation

1. A bounded Gulf Stream window is supported much more strongly than a single North-Central Gulf platform cell.
2. The Southern Coastal Segment remains strong at 0.5° and is close enough to Florida and the US East Coast to retain the project's practical framing.
3. Cape Hatteras has the strongest cell-level support, but it is the separation/turning region and should be treated as a harder dynamic test rather than the easiest first pilot.
4. The Northern Offshore Extension is data-rich but has weaker direct relevance to near-coast response and search scenarios.
5. Choosing the Southern Coastal Segment as a pilot validates the method on the Gulf Stream System. It does not by itself answer how a release in the North-Central Gulf reaches that segment; that remains a later transfer or full-chain question.

## Provisional recommendation

- **Initial study window:** Southern Coastal Segment.
- **Initial origin-cell resolution:** 0.5° is feasible inside this data-rich window; retain a 1° sensitivity baseline.
- **Stress-test window:** Cape Hatteras Separation.
- **Deferred sparse-domain test:** one North-Central Gulf source cell, with explicit uncertainty and no claim of equal accuracy.

This recommendation changes the role of North-Central Gulf from the first model-development region to a later application/transfer test. The team should confirm that this scope change still matches the intended proposal narrative.
