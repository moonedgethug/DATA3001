What changed, and what stayed fixed

ExactPoint uses the original historical endpoint atoms. EndpointCI spreads usable endpoints using their repaired 95% marginal half-widths divided by 1.9599639845, with fixed normal shape and zero axis correlation. MatchedSpread uses the same valid mask but a common query-level covariance. B2Point retains the original one-degree conditioning rule and exact endpoint coordinates.

Every historical donor keeps its original vote. Only endpoint uncertainty changes. No origin jitter, wind, velocity, bandwidth selection, Gaussian transport model or neural estimator is introduced.

A location CI is not a seven-day transport interval. The final forecast is a mixture of historical destinations. The 90% region is a common distribution-quantile ellipse, not a minimum-area region. Actual hit rate need not equal its nominal model probability.
